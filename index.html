SF Events

Subscribable calendars of San Francisco events, refreshed every 3 hours. Subscribe to the buckets you want; hide one by toggling its calendar off.

Alerts and invites: open an event, copy or duplicate it into your own calendar, then add an alert or invite people there. Subscribed calendars are read-only, and clients drop per-event changes on refresh.

Other apps: paste https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nredd/sf-cal/feeds/<feed>.ics into "subscribe by URL".
